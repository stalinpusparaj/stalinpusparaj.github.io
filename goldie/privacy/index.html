Goldie's Great Escape: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Goldie's Great Escape ("the game") is made by GEM CREATORS ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, scores, quests, streak, settings and owned items) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to show, measure and protect ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying "Remove ads" turns off banner and between-run ads.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see or store your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and restore them on your devices. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and a random app user ID that is not linked to your name or email. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Delete your data

Goldie's Great Escape (by GEM CREATORS) has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign out of.

  1. Game progress: uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Goldie's Great Escape → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes all your progress, coins and settings from the device.
  2. Advertising data: in Android Settings → Google → Ads, tap "Delete advertising ID" (or "Reset advertising ID").
  3. Purchase records: email gemmyapps@gmail.com with the subject "Goldie's Great Escape data deletion". We will ask RevenueCat to delete the purchase history linked to your app user ID within 30 days. Google Play keeps its own order records for legal and tax reasons.

Data retention

Data saved on your device is kept until you delete it as described above. Data held by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies.
