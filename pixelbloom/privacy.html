Privacy Policy

Pixel Bloom (app ID com.gemmyapps.pixelbloom) · Effective 2026-11-02

Summary

Pixel Bloom has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account of its own. Your progress is stored on your device. We do not run servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, completed levels, streaks, coins, settings and a random install ID (used only to merge coin counts between your own devices). This stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your game progress is saved to your Google account (Saved Games) so it can sync across devices, and your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Google Play Games. This is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete the cloud save from Play Games settings, or stop at any time by signing out.

Purchases

Optional purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play so it can deliver what you bought.

Sharing

When you choose to share a picture, video or link, the app hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is uploaded by us.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
