Bible Seasons: Daily Calendar: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Bible Seasons: Daily Calendar (package com.gemmyapps.bibleseasons) handles your information. The app is made by GEM CREATORS. Questions go to gemmyapps@gmail.com.

The short version

  • GEM CREATORS does not collect your data. We have no server for this app and we cannot see anything you put in it.
  • The app does not request internet access. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you enter is stored on your phone.
  • Your information can leave your phone in two ways: when you choose to share or print something, and through Android's own backup or device transfer. Details are below.

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • your first name, if you choose to give one
  • your church tradition, theme and daily reminder hour
  • journal entries and Daily Light notes, with their date and prompt
  • prayer intentions
  • family occasions you add, such as birthdays, baptisms and anniversaries
  • your calendar title, and how each photo is positioned on its page
  • photos you add to your calendar
  • alarm settings and sound mixes
  • your progress, such as streaks, leaves, practice counts and completed devotions

Alarm settings, tradition and theme are also kept in a separate part of the app's storage that Android can read before you first unlock the phone after a restart. This lets your alarm ring after a restart.

The app also keeps short-lived working files in its cache: a temporary copy of a photo while it is being added, and pictures or PDFs you are about to share. The temporary photo copy is deleted after use. Files made for sharing are cleared once they are more than an hour old, the next time the app runs and tidies up. A finished calendar PDF stays until the app is next restarted.

Photos

You choose photos with the system picker, one at a time. The app has no permission to browse your photo library and sees only the photo you pick.

The app reads the photo's orientation, then makes its own resized copy for your calendar. That copy does not keep the original's metadata, such as the location or the camera details. Your original photo is not changed.

If the photo you pick is stored in the cloud, the service that holds it delivers it to your phone. That service's own privacy policy applies to that step.

When your information can leave your phone

Sharing and printing, when you choose to. If you share a verse, a calendar page or a PDF, Android's share sheet opens and you choose where it goes. If you print, Android's print dialog opens and you choose the printer or print service. Calendar pages can include your photos, your calendar title and the names of your family occasions. Once shared, the file is handled by whoever or whatever you sent it to.

Android backup and device transfer. Android can back up app data to your own Google account, and can copy it to a new phone during device transfer. This is done by Android and Google, not by this app, and depends on your device settings. Your calendar photos are set to be left out of this. Everything else the app stores is not left out, so your journal, prayer intentions, first name, family occasions and settings can be included. GEM CREATORS cannot access these backups. Google's privacy policy applies to them, and you can turn backup off in your phone's settings. Depending on your phone, a backup may not have extra encryption if the phone has no screen lock.

Two related cases do not send what you put in the app. Reading aloud. Sessions and the alarm verse are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine on your phone, which is a separate app. The text sent to it is the app's own content, such as Scripture and session narration. Your journal, prayer intentions and name are not sent to it. How that engine works, including whether it uses a network connection, depends on the engine your phone uses and that engine's own terms.

Links and call buttons. The privacy policy link and the helpline link (findahelpline.com) open in your own browser. The call buttons open your phone app, with 988 filled in or with an empty keypad, and you place the call yourself. The app sends none of your information with them. The site or service you reach sees your visit or call as it would any other, your browser or phone may keep it in its history, and that service's own terms apply.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other data-collecting libraries. We do not sell or share your data. Google Play handles the download and installation of the app and has its own privacy terms.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: daily and bedtime reminders, and alarm, sound and export status. Android asks for your consent on recent versions.
  • Exact alarms and full-screen display: so your wake alarm rings on time and shows over the lock screen.
  • Foreground services: so sleep sounds, narration and the alarm keep playing, and a year calendar PDF keeps being made, while the app is in the background.
  • Keeping the phone awake: so those same tasks are not stopped partway.
  • Running after restart: so your alarms and daily reminder are set again after the phone restarts.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library.

How your information is protected

Your information is held in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. The app does not add its own encryption on top of that, so keeping a screen lock on your phone helps protect it. The app sends nothing over the internet itself.

How long information is kept, and how to delete it

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can remove individual photos, family occasions and prayer intentions in the app. Journal entries cannot be removed in the app, one at a time or all together. The app has no single button that deletes everything.

To delete everything on your phone, uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings, find Bible Seasons and clear its storage. A copy held in an Android backup is managed through your Google account and device backup settings, not by this app. If you uninstall and later reinstall, Android may restore your data from that backup.

Because we do not hold your data, we have nothing to delete on our side and no account to close.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that uses the internet, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
