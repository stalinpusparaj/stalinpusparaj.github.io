Privacy Policy

Light Up the Skies · Effective 12 October 2026

In short: the game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your settings, drawings, photos and videos stay on your device unless you choose to share them. If you subscribe to Premium, the app store and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, process that purchase.

This policy explains how the Light Up the Skies app for Android and iOS, and the web version of the game, handle information. "We" means the developer of Light Up the Skies.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. The game contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few things locally so it remembers you between visits: your settings (for example sound, quality and colour theme), the landmark you chose, your achievements, which crackers you have tried, and whether your Premium subscription is active. This stays on your device; delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data (on the web, by clearing the site's data in your browser).

Camera

AR mode uses your camera only while it is switched on, to show fireworks over your surroundings. The camera image is processed on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is only saved if you choose to take a photo or record a video.

Photos and videos

  • Saving: photos and videos you capture are saved to your device's gallery or offered in your device's share sheet. On iOS the app asks for permission to add them to your photo library.
  • Greeting-card photos: if you add a photo to a greeting card, you pick it with your device's photo picker. It is resized on your device and used only in that card.

Greeting-card links

When you share a greeting card, the recipient's name, your name, the message and any photo are encoded inside the link itself. They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers, but anyone who has the link can open it and see the card, so share it only with people you trust.

Premium subscriptions

Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payments are handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never see your payment details.

To verify, restore and manage subscriptions we use RevenueCat. When you open the subscription screen or buy Premium, the app sends RevenueCat an anonymous app user ID it generates, your purchase and subscription history, and basic technical information such as your device model, operating system and app version, country (from the store), and IP address. This is used only to provide and support the subscription. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Google Play or Apple ID subscription settings.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The web version does not offer purchases.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a subscription, contact us and we will help have it removed.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal information ourselves, most requests can be met by deleting the app's data on your device. For subscription records held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will request their access or deletion on your behalf. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, which you can exercise by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
