The game

Light Up the Skies is a fireworks game for phones, TVs and the web. It is for players aged 13 and over. The fireworks are on screen only: please follow your local rules and timings for real crackers.

Premium

  • Free trials and offers (such as a 7-day trial or a lower first month) are for new subscribers. When a trial ends, the plan's full price is charged unless you cancelled before then. An offer's later periods are charged at the plan's full price.

  • Restore Premium on a new device with the same Google Play account.

Gems

  • Gems are an in-game currency for the bigger crackers. They have no cash value, can't be exchanged or transferred, and live on your device.
  • Gem packs are one-time purchases through Google Play. A payment that is still processing (for example by UPI) is credited automatically once it completes, so there is no need to buy again.
  • Offers such as the starter pack or the Diwali box show their end date in the game. Prices are always the ones Google Play shows before you confirm.
